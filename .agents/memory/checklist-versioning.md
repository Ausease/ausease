---
name: Checklist versioning
description: Versioned checklist standards are published to explicit store and role audiences without rewriting completed work.
---

Published checklist versions are immutable. A new standard is a new version with its own steps, evidence rule, effective date, and store/role assignments; readers receive the highest effective version assigned to their store and role.

**Why:** Updating a checklist in place would make historical completion records appear to refer to a different operating standard and could expose a future standard before its effective date.

**How to apply:** Keep checklist definitions and assignments separate from daily routine instances and completion evidence. Require headquarters authorization for creation, versioning, and publication, and enforce organization scope on every read and write.
